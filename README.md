> A curated list of resources for traveling and cultural research in Ukraine, covering official state portals, national rail networks, cultural heritage guides, and diplomatic travel advisories.

Ukraine is a resilient Eastern European destination featuring rich cultural heritage, historic architectural monuments, vibrant urban centers, and extensive natural river valleys. This list is intended for cultural researchers, heritage historians, independent travelers, and documentary explorers.

## Official & Planning

Essential official government portals, cultural heritage archives, digital state services, and weather tracking.

- [Ukraine.ua (Official Website of Ukraine)](https://ukraine.ua/) - Official state portal managed by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs featuring culture, history, resilient communities, and digital resources.
- [State Agency for Tourism Development of Ukraine (SATD)](https://www.tourism.gov.ua/) - Official government agency promoting cultural heritage, architectural preservation, and regional tourism standards.
- [Diia (State Portal of Ukraine)](https://diia.gov.ua/) - Official digital government platform showcasing Ukraine's e-governance infrastructure and digital identification systems.
- [Ukrainian Hydrometeorological Center](https://meteo.gov.ua/) - Official national weather service providing meteorological forecasts, climate data, and environmental tracking.

## Transportation & Rail

Navigating national railway networks, intercity electric trains, and transit planning.

- [Ukrzaliznytsia (Ukrainian Railways - UZ)](https://www.uz.gov.ua/en/) - National passenger railway operator managing Intercity+ high-speed electric trains and intercity routes connecting Kyiv with regional hubs.
- [Kyivpastrans (Kyiv Public Transit)](https://kpt.kyiv.ua/) - Official municipal transport operator managing Kyiv's metro lines, trams, trolleybuses, and urban funicular.

## Destinations & Cultural Heritage

### Kyiv & Central Region

- [Visit Kyiv (Official City Portal)](https://kyivcity.gov.ua/) - Official municipal guide for exploring Kyiv's UNESCO-listed heritage sites, including Saint Sophia Cathedral and the Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra.
- [Ministry of Culture and Information Policy - Heritage Protection](https://mcip.gov.ua/) - Official government portal managing national museums, historical reserves, and architectural landmarks.

### Lviv & Western Region

- [Lviv City Portal (Lviv.travel)](https://lviv.travel/) - Official municipal visitor guide for exploring Lviv's UNESCO-listed medieval Old Town, historic coffee culture, and architectural monuments.

### Odesa & Black Sea Coast

- [Odesa City Official Portal](https://omr.gov.ua/en/) - Official municipal portal for Odesa's historic 19th-century architecture, Potemkin Stairs, and Black Sea cultural heritage.

## Safety & Diplomatic Advisories

Important consular warnings, emergency contact networks, and travel advisory resources.

- [Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Ukraine - Travel Advice](https://mfa.gov.ua/) - Official consular portal providing entry regulations, border crossing updates, and diplomatic mission directories.
- [State Emergency Service of Ukraine (SESU)](https://www.dsns.gov.ua/) - National emergency response agency operating civil protection, rescue services, and public safety alerts.
